# [Spike title, phrased as the question]

Write this README the way you would say it out loud to a teammate. Short sentences. "We" and "I". Contractions are fine. Use sentences where a bullet fragment would sound like a slide. Fill in each part below in your own words, and delete these instructions.

## What question were we trying to answer?
Say it in one sentence, as a question.

## Why did we do this?
Tell the story in a few sentences. What were we about to build, and what worried us?

## What did we look at?
Say what you read and tried. For example: "I read the vendor docs, checked how the repo already does this, and then built a small test."

## What did we learn?
Tell the findings in order, as a short paragraph or a few numbered sentences. Each one should make sense when read aloud on its own.

## What is the proof?
Say what you built and what you ran, then paste the real output. After the output, say in a sentence what it shows and what it doesn't.

## What options did we consider?
For each option, say what it is, what's good about it, and what's bad about it, in plain sentences.

## What do we recommend?
Start with "I'd recommend", then say what to do in the real build and why. Point back to the proof.

## What could go wrong, and what don't we know?
Say it plainly. "We didn't test X." "We still don't know Y."

## What happens next?
Say what should happen after this: stories to create, more research, or the real build.

## What is the answer?
Answer the first question in one or two sentences. Say the verdict (VALIDATED, PARTIAL, INVALIDATED or INCONCLUSIVE) and what it means for the decision.
